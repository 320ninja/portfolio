Safar Travel

A ready-made booking app that travel agencies rebrand as their own. Each tour has a day-by-day plan with map routes, stops, hotels and private notes. All prices switch between USD and NPR with one tap. It runs as a PHP web app (PWA) on ordinary hosting.

  • Tap USD / NPR to switch every price
  • Open a tour and step through the itinerary days
  • Write a private note for a day, and it saves itself
  • Book with dates and travellers, then check My trips
