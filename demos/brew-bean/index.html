Our story

From a tea stall
to your table.

It started with one copper kettle and a bench outside our aunt’s house in Patan. Eleven years later we still brew every pot of chiya by hand, grind spices each morning, and fold every momo in the open kitchen where you can watch.

Our tea comes from a single co-op in Ilam, our buffalo meat from a family farm in Kirtipur, and our recipes from three generations of Newar home cooks.
