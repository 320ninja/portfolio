Ghar Real Estate

A property app for Nepal that buyers and sellers can install on their phones. It has listings with photos, bidding with a live countdown, distance filters, a WhatsApp contact button and a built-in assistant called "Ghar Guru". It runs as a PHP and SQLite web app (PWA) on ordinary hosting.

  • Search or filter by Buy / Rent, type and distance
  • Open a 🔥 HOT listing and place a bid
  • Switch to Seller and post a property
  • Ask Ghar Guru "3 bedroom house in Kathmandu"
