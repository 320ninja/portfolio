Features

More than just keys.

Web Audio Synthesis

Pure oscillator synthesis with ADSR envelope shaping — no samples, no latency, just clean tone generation directly in the browser.

Convolution Reverb

A synthetic impulse response creates natural room ambience. Enable it for a richer, more resonant sound.

Chord Recognition

Play two or more notes and the app names the chord in real time — major, minor, seventh, suspended, diminished and augmented.

Full Keyboard Mapping

Your laptop keyboard covers two full octaves. Shift up and down with Z/X to reach all 88 keys.
